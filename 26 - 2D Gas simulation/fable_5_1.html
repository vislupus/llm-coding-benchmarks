Measurements
What is being computed

Temperature. In 2D each particle has two translational degrees of freedom, so equipartition gives ⟨½mv²⟩ = kBT. We define T = (1/N)Σ½mivi². Light and heavy species should share the same T once equilibrated.

Pressure. Every wall hit delivers impulse 2m|v⊥|. P = Σimpulse / (Δt · perimeter), a force per unit length (2D pressure). The ideal-gas law in 2D reads P·A = N kBT. At finite packing fraction η = Σπr²/A, hard disks exceed it; the Henderson equation of state Z = (1 + η²/8)/(1 − η)² is shown for comparison.

Distributions. 2D Maxwell–Boltzmann speeds: f(v) = (mv/T)e−mv²/2T; components are Gaussian with σ² = T/m; kinetic energy is exponential, f(E) = e−E/T/T, identical for every species.

H-function. H = ∫ f ln f d²v, estimated from the speed histogram. Boltzmann's H-theorem says it decreases to its equilibrium value ln(m/2πT) − 1 (per species). Switch collisions off and start with equal speeds: H stays put.

Collisions. Exact two-body elastic solution (momentum and energy conserved to floating-point precision); overlaps from the finite time step are removed by sliding disks apart along the line of centres, which does not alter velocities. The step is subdivided so no disk travels more than ~⅓ of a radius per sub-step.
