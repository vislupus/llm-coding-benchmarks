How to play

Gestures of the craft

W A S DMove. You are fast; keep moving. SpaceJump, and jump again in mid-air. Jump over shockwave rings. ShiftDash. Brief invulnerability, recharging pips. Left mousePrimary cast of the active weave (hold to keep casting). Right mouseHold to charge the weave's alternate cast, release to unleash. 1 · 2 · WheelSwap between your two weaves. ELeft-hand sigil. QCataclysm, when the ring is full. Fills as you deal damage. TabShow your build. EscPause.

Reactions. Each weave leaves a mark: Ember burns, Rime chills, Volt shocks, Gloam hexes. Strike a marked foe with a different element to trigger a reaction such as Thermal Shock or Superconduct. Carry two weaves and swap often.
