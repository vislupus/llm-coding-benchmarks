    The Long Night

    The Gloam rises from the blighted hollows each dusk. Raise a kingdom by daylight, hold the walls by lantern-light, then march out and burn their nests before the dark swallows the vale.

    Keeping the vale

    Villagers gather on their own once assigned in the Labor panel. Build by day, defend by night, and push out to destroy the Gloam nests. Everything the Gloam sends grows stronger with each night.

    Tips

    • Build only inside your territory (the blue glow while placing). Banner Posts extend it.
    • Walls are drag-placed. The Gloam bashes through the weakest point, so pair walls with Watchtowers.
    • Spearguard deal heavy damage to large foes; Shieldbearers hold the line; Longbows and Emberseers belong behind them.
    • A warning names the direction of the next attack. The Gloam probes for the side you defend least.
    • Vision shrinks at night unless you research Lantern Watch.
