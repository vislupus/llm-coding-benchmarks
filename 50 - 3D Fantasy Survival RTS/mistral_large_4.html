🔥 Onslaught

Relentless economy, brutal night raids, elite-heavy waves.

Goal: destroy the enemy Dark Keep (top-right). Lose your own Keep and the realm falls.
Camera: WASD/arrows pan · wheel zoom · Q/E or middle-drag rotate · Space jump to alerts.
Select: left-click / drag-box (Shift adds) · Ctrl+1-9 set group, 1-9 recall.
Command: right-click move / attack / gather · A then click = attack-move · S stop · B build menu · R rotate wall.
Gather 🪙🪵🌾 with villagers (they auto-gather when idle), build with villagers, research at the Blacksmith. Capture ⚜ shrines for gold income.
Survive the waves — they grow every time, and night raids are deadlier. 🔥

How to Play

Economy: Villagers gather gold from crystal rocks, wood from trees, food from berry bushes (bushes deplete). Farms trickle food. All goods drop at your Keep (wood also at Lumber Camps). Captured shrines grant +gold.
Build: Select a villager → B or the Build button → click a site. Lumber Camps must be near trees.
Army: Barracks → Footmen · Archery Range → Archers · Blacksmith → research · Stable → Knights (needs Barracks+Blacksmith) · Arcane Spire → Mages. Towers and walls defend.
Hero: Sir Caelan buffs nearby allies and respawns at your Keep.
Controls: LMB select/drag · RMB move-attack-gather · A attack-move · S stop · Ctrl+1-9/1-9 groups · Space last alert · P pause · wheel zoom · Q/E rotate.
Tip: Waves escalate — push out and destroy the Dark Keep before the night gets long.
