The Last Hearth

The Hollow has swallowed the vale. One hearth still burns. Gather, build and hold the light through the nights — then carry it to the Spires.

Win by destroying every Hollow Spire, or by enduring 12 nights. You lose if your Hearthkeep falls.

Your kingdom

  • Villagers gather food, wood, stone and gold. Use the Workforce panel (+ / −) to assign idle villagers instantly.
  • Select villagers to open the build menu. Buildings must go inside your golden territory — raise Banner Posts and upgrade the Keep to expand it.
  • Cottages raise your population. Farmsteads give endless food. Lodges and camps shorten the walk for gatherers.

Days and nights

  • By day the Hollow sends raids at your weakest outskirts. At night full waves march on you, larger every night, with elites and colossi later on.
  • Walls, gates and towers funnel and thin the horde. Ring the Hearth Bell to shelter villagers inside the Keep.

Command

  • Left-drag select · Right-click move / attack / gather / build · Shift add to selection · double-click selects all of a type.
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold ground · F cycle formation · Z/X hero abilities · G Hearth Bell
  • Ctrl+1-5 set group, 1-5 recall · . idle villager · Space jump to selection · Del demolish
  • Camera: arrow keys or screen edges to pan, wheel to zoom, Q/E rotate, middle-drag to pan. Touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap to select or command.
