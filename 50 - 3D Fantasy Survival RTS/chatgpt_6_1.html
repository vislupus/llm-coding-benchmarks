THE DAWNWARDEN’S FIELD GUIDE

A kingdom worth keeping.

Build a living economy

Tenders harvest trees, stone, amber seams and sunberries. They carry resources to the Hearthkeep or a Storehouse. Right-click a resource with Tenders selected to assign them. New Tenders start idle.

Sunmeadows need up to three workers. Cottages add 10 population. Waystones extend your territory and sight; they also earn coin. Builders return to their former jobs after construction.

Command the dawn

Recruit Wardens, Reedbows and Thornlancers from a Watchhall. Upgrade the Hearthkeep to II and build a Starforge for Glimmer sages. Living runes research unlocks elite Dawnforged.

Raids target exposed holdings. Nightfall brings a growing wave from the surviving Cinder Spires. Destroying all three wins the campaign. Ilyen’s Dawnpulse heals allies and stuns nearby enemies.
