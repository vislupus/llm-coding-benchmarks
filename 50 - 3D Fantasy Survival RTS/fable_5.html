EMBERHOLD

Raise a kingdom by day. Hold the ember through the night.

Goal. Destroy every Gloom Rift on the isle before your Great Keep falls. Each dusk, the Gloomspawn march.

Camera. WASD / arrows pan · Q,E rotate · wheel zoom · middle-drag pan · minimap click jumps.

Command. Left-drag selects · right-click moves, attacks, or sends workers to trees, rock, and gold · A + click = attack-move · F cycles formation.

Economy. Recruit workers at the Keep, right-click them onto resources or a Grain Croft. Houses raise your population cap. Storecairns shorten hauling trips.

Territory. You may only build inside your banners' reach — the Keep, War Banners, and Warden Towers extend it.

War. Muster Hall trains Shieldwardens, Fletcher's Roost trains Longstriders, the Rune Spire trains Runeweavers. Research at the Elder Sanctum. Your hero, the Flamewarden, rallies at the Keep (H) and unleashes a Flame Nova (R).

Groups. Ctrl+1..4 store control groups · 1..4 recall · Esc cancels · Space centers on the Keep.
