How it works

Each step is one Monte Carlo sweep: plants regrow logistically on a heterogeneous fertility map, then individuals are drawn at random (about one draw per animal) and each stochastically pays metabolism, senses by sampling random cells within its vision radius, moves, feeds, and may reproduce. Reproduction is suppressed by local crowding and costs half the parent's energy. Offspring inherit speed, vision and reproduction rate with Gaussian mutations; speed and vision cost energy, so traits trade off against survival. Watch for predator–prey cycles, arms races in speed and vision, and extinctions after overshoot — then try a catastrophe or seasons.
