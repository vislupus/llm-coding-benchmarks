Supported Formulas & Shortcuts

Interactive Formula Point-and-Click

Start typing a formula with = in the formula bar or cell (e.g. =SUM( or =), then click or drag across cells on the grid to insert their addresses automatically.

Supported Functions & Operators

Arithmetic: +, -, *, /, ^ (power), ()
Range Functions: SUM(A1:A10), AVERAGE(A1:A10), MIN(A1:A10), MAX(A1:A10), COUNT(A1:A10), COUNTA(A1:A10), MEDIAN(A1:A10), PRODUCT(A1:A5)
Logic & Math: IF(A1>100, "High", "Low"), ROUND(A1, 2), ABS(A1), SQRT(A1)
Text: CONCAT(A1, " ", B1), UPPER(A1), LOWER(A1), LEN(A1)

Keyboard Shortcuts

Arrows / Tab / Enter
Navigate across cells
Shift + Arrows
Extend selection range
Double-Click or F2
Edit active cell
Delete / Backspace
Clear contents of selected range
Ctrl/Cmd + C / V
Copy / Paste cells (adjusts relative formulas)
Ctrl/Cmd + Z / Y
Undo / Redo
Ctrl/Cmd + B / I / U
Toggle Bold, Italic, Underline
