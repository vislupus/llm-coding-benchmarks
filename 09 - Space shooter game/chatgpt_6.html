FLIGHT MANUAL

Stay alive, destroy invaders, and push your personal best. Each sector lasts 32 seconds; every third sector is guarded by a capital ship. Destroy it to advance.

MOVE & FIREWASD or arrow keys to steer. Hold Space to fire. Auto-fire starts enabled; toggle it with F. You can also hold and drag on the game field.
TOUCH CONTROLSDrag anywhere on the field to steer. Movement is relative, so your finger can stay away from the ship. Auto-fire lets you focus on dodging.
NOVA SHOCKWAVEPress E or tap NOVA. It shatters asteroids, clears hostile shots, and damages aliens. Recharges in 18 seconds.
PAUSE & SOUNDP or Escape pauses. M toggles sound. The game pauses when you leave the tab. Use the FX button to reduce particles and remove screen shake.

Pickups & scoring

  • Blue shield: 7 seconds of protection.
  • Yellow bolt: 10 seconds of rapid, triple-shot fire.
  • Green star: repairs one hull segment.
  • Chain kills within 3.5 seconds to build a score multiplier, up to ×5. Getting hit resets your combo.
  • Large asteroids split into dangerous fragments. Your ship has four hull segments. Every second cleared sector repairs one segment.
  • Difficulty changes enemy speed and attack rate. The personal best is stored separately for each flight mode.
