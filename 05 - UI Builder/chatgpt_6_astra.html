Give your ideas a little form.

A complete design workbench, in one offline file.

01 / Place and shape

Drag a component from the library onto the canvas, or click it to add at the center. Select any element and drag its eight handles to resize. Use the round handle to rotate. Hold Shift to keep proportions. Shift-click or drag across empty canvas to select several elements.

02 / Make it precise

Snap uses the grid spacing in the toolbar. Smart guides align edges and centers with other elements and the canvas. Use the properties panel for exact position, dimensions, typography, corners, colors, shadows, and control values. Align or distribute multiple selections there.

03 / Connect the dots

Choose Connect, then click a source and destination. Or drag between the four purple attachment ports. Connections stay attached as elements move, rotate, or resize. Select a line to edit its route, anchors, arrows, label, and bend. Drag the purple route handle to adjust its path.

04 / Try it and take it

Preview activates inputs, dropdowns, sliders, steppers, toggles, and button actions. Export HTML creates a complete runnable design with all styles and connections. Save JSON to keep editing later. File → Download editor with this design creates a portable copy of the entire builder.

The canvas uses absolute positions. Exported designs scale to fit smaller screens while keeping your layout. Exports include real browser controls and optional message or visibility actions; server-side forms and application backends are outside this visual builder. Polygon shadows follow their shape; inset shadows apply to rectangular surfaces.
