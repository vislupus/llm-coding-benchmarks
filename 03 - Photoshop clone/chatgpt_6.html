Layers: click to select, drag to reorder, or use the arrows. Double-click a name to rename. Eye icons toggle visibility. Locks protect pixels and layer properties. Paint is clipped by an active selection.

Adjustments: brightness, contrast, color, blur, sharpening, and presets remain editable per layer. Blend modes, opacity, and drop shadows combine layers without changing their source pixels.

Touch: paint with one finger; use two fingers to pan and pinch. Open the layer panel using the header button. Use the Hand tool for one-finger panning.

Files, limits, and conventions

Open PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, or the first frame of a GIF. Drop images to place them as layers. Prism projects (.prism) preserve source pixels, text, offsets, visibility, blending, adjustments, and the current selection; session history is not saved. PSD import/export and Adobe plug-ins are not supported.

Documents are limited to 4096 pixels per side and 16 megapixels, with 32 layers. Larger imported images are scaled down. History keeps up to 50 steps within an approximate 80 MB compressed-image budget. Changes are held in memory until you save or export; closing the page discards unsaved work.

Filters apply to the whole selected layer. Painting, fills, erasing, and copying respect selections. Layer moves and transformations affect the whole layer. Transformations rasterize text. Crop and canvas-size edits preserve pixels beyond the canvas; image exports include only its visible bounds.

Prism is an independent browser image editor. It does not use Adobe software, branding, or services.
