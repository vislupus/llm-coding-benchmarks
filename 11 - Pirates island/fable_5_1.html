⚓ Isle Guard

Enemy raiders are coming for the island. Command your three ships, sink them before they reach the shore, and spend plunder on upgrades between waves.

  • 1 / 2 / 3 or click a ship to select it; W A S D (or arrows) to steer; click the water to sail there.
  • Ships fire automatically at enemies in range (the yellow circle). Space fires manually, F toggles auto-fire.
  • Enemies that reach the shore bombard the island. If island HP hits 0, the game is over.
  • Between waves: buy upgrades, repair, then press N or the button to start the next wave.
