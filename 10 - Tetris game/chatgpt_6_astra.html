READY WHEN YOU ARE
A little focus. A fresh start.

Use Left and Right arrows or A and D to move, Up or X or W to rotate clockwise, Z to rotate counterclockwise, Down or S to soft drop, Space to hard drop, C or Shift to hold, and P or Escape to pause. Enter starts, resumes, or restarts after game over. Fill a horizontal row to clear it. Every ten lines increases the level and falling speed. The game pauses when this tab loses focus. Touch controls are available below the board on small or touch screens.
