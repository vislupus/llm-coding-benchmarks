Pagoda of the
Coiling Dragon

Eight storeys of vermilion timber and glazed teal tile rise from a marble terrace, each eave flaring upward beneath a fan of radiating ridges. A jade dragon turns through the garden — its body passing behind the upper tiers and re-emerging across the terrace in front — jaws open beside a flaming pearl.

Pine and scholar rock to the left, bamboo to the right, lotus on still water below: the four motifs of the classical literati garden.
