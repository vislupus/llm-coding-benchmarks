Play

Tap one of your pieces: gold dots appear on quiet squares, crimson rings on captures. Tap a marked square to move. On the last rank you choose the promoted piece.

Camera

Drag to orbit · wheel or pinch to zoom · ⟳ Flip (or F) swings the view to the other side · ⟲ recentres.

Controls

U undo your last move and the AI's reply · P flip · difficulty can be changed mid-game and applies to the AI's next move.

The engine

Iterative-deepening negamax with alpha-beta pruning, MVV-LVA capture ordering, piece-square tables and quiescence search. It thinks in small slices between frames so the board never freezes; the chip shows live depth and node counts.

Draws

Stalemate, fifty-move rule, threefold repetition and insufficient material are all detected automatically.
