Read the world.

Hold the left trigger to draw a teleport arc. Release over the landing ring to move. The right thumbstick snap-turns by 30°. Smooth locomotion, when selected, uses the left thumbstick and dims the edges of your view while moving.

Hold the right trigger on a plant, creature, ruin, or core to scan it. Squeeze a controller’s grip near a small object to hold it; release to drop or throw. A held core can be scanned directly with the right trigger.

Carry a little light.

The left wrist carries your field journal. Point at its buttons with the right controller and press its trigger. The right thumbstick click shows or hides the journal. Settings, a hand lamp, a return to the observatory, and core recall are available inside VR.

Restore the cyan, violet and amber cores at the observatory. Approach grazing creatures slowly; a gentle touch may reveal more than a scan. Explore freely after the observatory awakens.

Opening a headset menu or losing tracking releases held objects and cancels pending actions. When tracking returns, release and press triggers or grips again; center the thumbsticks before moving or turning.
