Pure WebXR // Immersive VR

XENO-AETHER

Step onto an uncharted bioluminescent exoplanet. Explore crystalline caverns, glowing fungal valleys, obsidian monolith ruins, reactive alien flora, and ancient harmonic artifacts.

Locomotion & Flight

Left Stick: Walk / Strafe across terrain.
Right Stick L/R: Snap Turn.
Right Stick Up / A-X Button: Low-G Jetpack Thrust.

Scanner & Wrist Radar

Trigger: Emit topographic sonic scan wave & reveal artifact beacons.
Left Wrist Holo: 3D directional needles pointing to all 5 Artifacts.

Physical Interactions

Grip / Squeeze: Grab & carry glowing Artifacts (or tele-pull from 2.5m). Place all 5 cores into the Central Ruin Pedestals.

Living Ecosystem

Touch Flora: Brush glowing mushrooms & crystals to make them bloom and chime.
B-Y Button: Cycle planetary weather anomalies.
