      How realistic is this model?
      • Sizes are not to scale. A real cell is about 20 µm across; ribosomes (25 nm) and molecules are drawn hundreds of times larger so you can see them.
      • Timing is compressed. Making and secreting one protein takes 20 minutes or more; mitosis takes about an hour. Here each takes seconds.
      • Only a few hundred particles are shown. A real cell holds billions of proteins and millions of ribosomes.
      • Paths are scripted from textbook pathways rather than simulated from physics; motion is diffusion-like jitter layered on those paths.
      • The ER, Golgi and actin cortex are simplified. Actin filaments, intermediate filaments and peroxisomes are not shown.
      • The cell is shown with 3 chromosome pairs (humans have 23) already copied, as in the G2 phase.
