How to play

Surround more of the board than your opponent. A group with no empty points next to it is captured. You can't immediately retake a single stone in a ko, and you can't play a move that leaves your own stones without liberties. When both players pass in a row, the board is counted.

Place a stone
Click an intersection. On touch screens, tap (twice if confirmation is on).
Look around
Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or shift-drag to pan.
Counting
After two passes, tap any group to switch it between dead and alive, then accept the result.
Keys
P pass, U undo, H hint, E estimate, N new game, T top view, R reset view, arrows orbit, M mute
