How to load the file & how the numbers are calculated

Load the local CSV

Keep this HTML and temperature_Sofia_2809794_1952-01-01 - 2021-11-28.csv in the same folder. The page automatically attempts to read that exact relative filename.

If you opened the HTML by double-clicking it, your browser may block access to a neighboring file. Use Open CSV or drop the CSV onto this page. File selection reads it locally and does not upload it.

For automatic loading, you can serve that folder locally:

python3 -m http.server 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000/sofia-climate-dashboard.html. Chart.js is loaded from a version-pinned CDN and requires internet access. CSV data is never sent to that CDN.

Accepted format

The required headers are DATE, TAVG, TMAX, and TMIN. Headers are case-insensitive. Quoted fields, UTF-8 BOM, CRLF, comma/semicolon/tab separators, ISO dates, and YYYYMMDD dates are supported.

All temperature values are divided by 10, as specified for this file. This is a fixed unit conversion, not automatic unit detection.

Means, extremes, and counts

The overall mean is the observation-weighted mean of valid daily TAVG values in the selected years. Annual and monthly means average the available daily values independently for each temperature field. TAVG is never replaced by (TMAX + TMIN) / 2.

Seasonal view pools matching calendar months across the selected years; Timeline shows every individual year-month, including gaps. Annual extremes are max(TMAX) and min(TMIN). Hot/cold counts use inclusive thresholds of 30°C / −10°C. Counts are observed totals, not estimates for missing days.

Estimated trend

Ordinary least squares fits annual TAVG against calendar year, reported in °C per decade. A year is eligible only with at least 90% of its daily TAVG values and at least 80% in every month. At least three eligible years are required. Incomplete years remain visible in the charts and statistics. A negative result indicates cooling in the selected record. This descriptive trend is not a forecast or an attribution analysis.

Missing and questionable records

Blank, NA, N/A, NULL, NaN, M, --, −9999, and 9999 values are treated as missing. Non-numeric values and converted temperatures outside −100…100°C are excluded. Invalid dates and rows missing required fields are skipped. Repeated dates are combined: missing fields are filled, but the first valid value wins when values conflict. TMIN greater than TMAX invalidates that high/low pair. Multiple stations must be separated before loading.

A parsing report will appear here after loading. No sample temperatures are included in this dashboard.
